How it is laid out

Each category gets a hub placed on a sphere. Its tags sit in a small cloud around that hub, and a tag that appears in more outfits gets a bigger sphere. Lines between tags mean they appeared in the same outfit; the threshold above keeps a single outfit from drawing every pair. Tags not in the vocabulary go to a “custom” cluster. Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, hover or tap a node for details, click a node to pin it, click the legend to hide a category.
